Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MATECH BURUNDI, Rohero, Avenue de l'Amitié, Building ABC, Bujumbura, Burundi ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information in connection with BATA, our AI receptionist subscription service (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the name of your business, your contact name, email address and phone number, and the plan you subscribe to.
  • Business information you provide: your services, prices, opening hours, policies and answers to frequent questions, which BATA uses to reply to your customers.
  • Conversations processed for you: messages your customers exchange with your BATA receptionist on your website or WhatsApp, and the booking or contact details they provide (such as their name, dates and phone number). We process this information on your behalf so that the Service can answer and forward requests to you.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. We do not receive or store your full card details; Paddle provides us with confirmation of payment and subscription status.
  • Technical information: basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service, such as timestamps and message delivery status.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Service for your business.
  • To forward bookings, leads and conversations to you.
  • To respond to your support requests and service notifications.
  • To improve the quality and reliability of the Service, using aggregated and anonymised statistics.

We do not sell personal information to anyone. We do not use your customers' conversations to advertise to them, and we do not share them with third parties except the service providers needed to run the Service (such as messaging and hosting providers), under confidentiality obligations.

3. Your role and your customers

For the conversations BATA handles for your business, you are responsible for informing your customers, through your own channels, that an automated receptionist may answer them and that their requests are forwarded to you. We delete conversation data associated with your account when you close your account, subject to legal retention requirements.

4. Retention and security

We keep account information for as long as your subscription is active, and conversation records for up to 12 months to support reporting and dispute handling, after which they are deleted or anonymised. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures — access controls, encryption in transit, and limited staff access — to protect information against unauthorised access.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you and your business at any time by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. Your customers may address their requests to your business, and we will assist you in responding to them.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy from time to time; the current version is always published on this page with its "Last updated" date. For any privacy question or request, contact us at izererumeca@gmail.com.
