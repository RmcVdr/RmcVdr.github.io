Questions

Which languages does BATA speak?

French first — BATA was built for French-speaking businesses and their customers — and English. Your customers are answered in the language they write in.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

For the WhatsApp channel, yes: a WhatsApp Business number for your business. We guide you through the connection, step by step.

What if BATA does not know an answer?

BATA never invents information. If a question is outside your business information, it politely takes the customer's contact details and passes the conversation to your team.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There is no long-term contract. Cancel whenever you like and your receptionist stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.
